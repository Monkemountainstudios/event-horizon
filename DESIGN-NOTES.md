# Event Horizon design laws and future stages

## Governing laws

- Gravity determines how quickly death approaches.
- Integrity determines which transformations are physically eligible.
- Probability determines the particular history, while never reversing the arrow of time.
- Previous events change the conditions and likelihood of later events.
- Gravity makes death inevitable without requiring perfectly monotonic travel; a bounded impact may briefly trade radial energy and prolong one trajectory.
- Structure, spatial individuality, and recognisable identity collapse together.
- Visual objects are views of audio-engine state, not decorative visualiser particles.

Mass derived from relative chunk loudness is deliberately constrained to ±15% around the shared trajectory. It creates noticeable leadership and lag without dividing one captured phrase into radically different lifespans. The global Gravity control changes the scale of the entire universe; analysed mass only perturbs that common law.

## Spatial collapse

Entities begin with restrained spatial individuality and move in slow, followable stereo orbits. Fragments inherit and continuously follow their parent's current orbital pan rather than freezing at their launch coordinate. The display uses that same horizontal phase, so left/right screen position and heard stereo position describe one event. The available core field then narrows nonlinearly toward the centre. Near death, the surviving identity retains only a small central phase/pan movement, preventing the plasma body from becoming wide auto-pan.

The central body may nevertheless shed a secondary corona. A bounded selection of inner fragments is copied through a steep high-pass filter and placed much farther from centre, while the complete original remains in the narrow motorwash. The corona adds breadth and exposed spectral edges without homogenising, replacing, or prettifying the identity-bearing plasma.

### Grazing impacts and reversal

Still-coherent outer bodies make probabilistic contact when their radial and angular positions overlap. A grazing impact exchanges a small amount of angular momentum, pushes one body noticeably outward while nudging the other inward, and temporarily alters their orbital relationship. The outward body can never escape this way and immediately resumes its inevitable fall. Members of one phrase begin colliding only after their initial cohesion loosens, and do so more gently and less often than unrelated bodies. A stronger cross-phrase contact may reverse the orbit and playback direction of one complete phrase-family. Per-body and global cooldowns keep a populated field from becoming continuous percussion.

An impact also performs one transient reciprocal grain exchange: each trajectory may voice a fragment carrying the other recording's material. The source buffers themselves are never fused or rewritten, preserving spectral ancestry and bounded ownership. Collision sound remains part of the physical, filtered matter vocabulary; it never enters the crystalline Hawking-radiation vocabulary.

A near-central cross-phrase impact has a much smaller chance of catastrophic rupture. The lighter blob disappears without Hawking radiation; the heavier blob divides into two smaller red descendants carrying its own material. One continues at original speed while the other runs at half speed, producing a naturally octave-lowered, twice-longer shadow. Both remain gravitationally bound. Rupture has an independent three-to-six-minute cooldown, and reuses the destroyed runtime for the second descendant so it never expands the fixed real-time pools.

The same engine event supplies a short-lived visual energy value. A local orange-to-white arc flares at the true orbital coordinate while the central disc remains completely black. These flares are grinder-like contact around the rim, not escaping radiation.

### Retrograde and parabolic escape

Retrograde is a coherent state, not granular randomness: every detected part of an eligible phrase plays backwards and the complete visual cohort orbits in the opposite direction. It can be innate at admission or caused by a sufficiently energetic outer collision.

A rarer flyby is admitted as an intact passing object. Its captured phrase sounds once while its shared visual cohort crosses the stereo field on a curved path, approaches the black hole without entering the destructive zones, and exits the opposite side. Distance along the path shapes pan, level and ordinary reverb; it receives no added sample and no Hawking bloom.

A terminal slingshot is the still rarer mirror case. Instead of crossing the horizon, a dying generation may reverse, curl around the rim, play its own captured material backwards, and accelerate out of the field. Flybys and slingshots share a four-to-eight-minute escape cooldown. Retrograde has a separate two-to-four-minute cooldown and remains somewhat more probable. Probability is zero only while the relevant cooldown is active; whenever it has elapsed, every eligible ordinary phrase or collision again has a small non-zero chance.

The plasma annulus is energy-responsive rather than permanently theatrical. With no resident inner sound it is only a thin ember. Accumulated near-horizon activity makes it broader and hotter; impacts add short tangential showers patterned after grinding steel. Reference orbit rings are omitted from the finished treatment. The universe is viewed through a recessed, convex smoked-glass cover with edge vignette and restrained reflection, while the black-hole disc is repainted as absolute black above the optical treatment.

Each captured phrase is also a visual cohort. Its detected chunks begin as a compact ordered group—conceptually `(OOOO)`—rather than being distributed evenly around an orbit. No permanent line joins them. Shared subgroup motion lets local relationships survive after the whole phrase loosens, allowing forms such as `(OO) (OO)` before complete separation. The geometry must be derived from generation membership, recorded order, gravity, and current integrity.

Blob diameter also carries integrity. Analysed mass remains readable in the outer field, but every object shrinks smoothly and aggressively after the middle journey, reaching roughly 12% of its original diameter at the horizon. This visual loss is continuous rather than stepped at zone boundaries. An offset radial light and dark limb give each blob planetary depth while its existing coloured atmospheric halo remains intact.

## Hawking-radiation death bloom

The event horizon and its accumulated smear eventually eject Hawking-radiation-like remnants. In the instrument, these are heard as glittering shimmer reverb attached to selected dying grains: quiet, high-energy fireworks blooming from the destruction of recognisable matter.

Before emission, a chunk pauses at the event-horizon boundary in a distinct dwell state. Its level has settled near 25%, its shortened fragments lengthen into overlapping smear grains, and it contributes to the central drone for an individualised period roughly ten times longer than the former boundary crossing. Gravity continues to bias the dwell clock. During the dwell a restrained precursor can leak into the shimmer field. When that time expires, the chunk rapidly falls through the boundary; that transition—not merely high proximity—is the causal trigger for the full Hawking bloom.

This is a terminal consequence, not a global pretty-space effect:

- Shimmer emerges principally near the horizon and at death.
- Every death may release at least a trace, but bloom magnitude and shape remain probabilistic.
- Some objects produce a restrained glint; exceptional collapses can flare into a larger bloom.
- The material sent into the bloom comes from the object's own final grains, retaining a faint spectral fingerprint.
- The bloom can briefly rise while the dry/granular identity falls, smearing the existing smear still further.
- Stereo width is narrow at the physical centre even as spectral height, diffusion, and harmonic brightness expand.
- A shared shimmer/diffusion bus should serve all entities; do not instantiate a reverb per chunk or grain.
- Sends, simultaneous blooms, feedback, pitch-shift voices, and tail energy must be strictly bounded.
- Major blooms impose a long event cooldown so terminal fireworks remain meaningful.

### Two separate shared reverberation systems

The ordinary gravity reverb and Hawking-radiation shimmer are separate buses. Both may sound physically large, but they serve different narrative and spectral roles.

**Gravity reverb** is a continuous environment. Its send rises with proximity, gradually replacing recognisable direct sound with depth, diffusion, and smear. It should remain comparatively natural or dark and must not introduce shimmer from birth.

**Hawking shimmer** is an event response. A terminal or near-terminal event selects individual grains and sends them through a dedicated radiation path:

```text
selected dying grain
    → octave/fifth energy transformation
    → short pre-delay
    → steep high-pass filtering near 5 kHz
    → long shimmer/diffusion field
    → centre-born outward radiation
```

The pitch transformation should normally precede the high-pass filter so that darker source material can still generate radiation. Treat 5 kHz as a starting value rather than a law; an internal range of roughly 3.5–7 kHz may prove more musical across different sources. A short pre-delay—initially around 50–150 ms—separates the death from its emitted bloom and makes the radiation causally recognisable rather than merely part of the same reverb tail.

The Hawking bus is silent until fed by an eligible event. Its output may outlive the object that caused it, but it remains gain-limited, feedback-limited, and voice-limited. Ordinary reverb communicates increasing depth; Hawking shimmer punctuates actual destruction. Most deaths are brief punctuation, while a bounded minority may become long ruptures with approximately twice the source-bearing duration and pre-delay. This variation changes the scale of a death without making radiation more frequent.

### Radiation is allowed to move outward

Matter and fragments only move inward. Hawking radiation is the deliberate exception because it is no longer treated as matter. Each emitted shimmer voice begins at dead centre after its pre-delay, receives a random outward destination, and blooms toward that coordinate as it decays. In stereo, the coordinate is a pan target anywhere up to the full left/right field; a future surround implementation could generalise the same state to an angle or spatial vector.

The motion should read as expansion rather than auto-panning:

```text
collapsed centre → widening luminous bloom → peripheral evaporation
```

Use a smooth outward envelope rather than a linear pan sweep. Several grains from one death may choose different destinations, creating a firework whose shared origin is perceptible. The identifiable spark may travel toward its target while the diffuse reverb tail simultaneously grows in stereo width. Radiation never falls back inward; it disappears at or before the edge. Its visual particle must use the same origin, trajectory, destination, age, and decay state as the audio voice.

The intended opposition is:

```text
physical space collapses  →  spectral space expands
recognisable matter dies  →  glittering radiation escapes
```

The first bounded implementation uses a shared high-passed shimmer reverb, a small fixed radiation-voice pool, 50–150 ms event pre-delay, octave/fifth source playback, and centre-to-random-edge panning. The display reads those same radiation voices after their real pre-delay and draws short blue-white lightning from dead centre toward the matching horizontal destination. Later versions may replace the simple raised-speed spark with a time-preserving spectral pitch shifter.

The first chain-reaction stage uses unequal stereo feedback delays ahead of the shimmer reverb. Cross-feedback allows one emission to seed subsequent outward reflections without permitting runaway gain. Feedback, tail time, and the number of simultaneously active radiation sources remain bounded even when several chunks collapse close together.

## Insert and continuous-performance modes

The instrument provides an equal-power dry/wet control when used as a DAW insert. Dry signal remains sample-aligned with the direct path; the evolving universe is the wet signal, including both gravity and Hawking buses.

The first continuous-capture mode listens while the performer keeps playing. Phrase detection creates related generations from the live stream without interrupting input monitoring. New material first returns with its original timing, then gravity progressively releases its chunks into independent trajectories. A 100 ms pre-roll protects the attack and roughly 180 ms of quiet closes a phrase. Silence remains the authoritative ending. LIVE assigns each admitted phrase a soft maximum between roughly two and six seconds: a starved machine modestly favours a longer mouthful, while a satisfied machine tends shorter. Manual recording remains four seconds and loaded material may use the full six-second allocation. Six generations may sound simultaneously and two further source slots may hold captured phrases at the outer boundary; beyond that, the machine waits rather than queueing unbounded audio or stealing existing matter.

Admission and phrase identity are separate concerns. The detector tracks a phrase even when no source slot is available. If capacity is full at the genuine onset, that phrase is skipped as a whole; a newly freed slot must not cause LIVE to capture only its sustained tail or final release. Noise-adaptive onset/release thresholds allow the gate to close reliably without toggling LIVE, and the next onset is then independently eligible.

Appetite is a probability bias, not a capture guarantee. Admission combines the `MAGNITUDE` macro's appetite component, current occupancy, and onset strength, then clamps the result away from both zero and certainty. An emptier universe tends to accept material; a crowded universe develops taste. Increasing source and chunk capacity must not automatically increase expensive synthesis density: six active generations and two waiting slots continue to compete for the fixed 192-fragment and 32-radiation-voice pools.

### Empty-universe plasma priming

Startup continuity is allowed one bounded exception to the normal journey. When no matter is active, the first admitted phrase may create up to three representative echo-copies that begin partway inward and descend rapidly. The complete source cohort still follows the ordinary outer path. Priming disables itself as soon as the universe is populated, so it shortens only the initial wait for historical depth and does not make the mature ecology busier per second.

## User-shaped temporal scale

The intact phase is counted in perceptible returns as well as seconds. The first reply remains near captured level, the first five launches establish identity while descending toward roughly 75%, and inward acceleration is restrained until that history exists. The initial return period is internally calibrated to 12 seconds. A single smooth ratio-based momentum curve then approaches granular cadence across most of the inward journey, avoiding an abrupt zone-boundary leap from looping to hyperspeed. The turbulent final ring inherits the gathered momentum and makes only the last bounded increase.

`CHURN` defines the base duration of the event-horizon residency. Per-chunk gravity and random identity still modify it, so the control changes the scale of the ecology without forcing every object to die in synchrony.

## Pre-plasma loss

The last granular orbit is a turbulent transition rather than a perfectly smooth acceleration into tone. Launch intervals wander, some intervals open into longer gaps, and increasingly damaged fragments can contain wider silent cuts. These absences are matter disappearing early: omitted material is pulled through without residue, echo, or radiation. Only the surviving object's terminal collapse is eligible for Hawking radiation. The chaos envelope recedes again at the boundary so the final plasma can become continuous.

## Plasma energy equality

Captured loudness belongs to recognisable matter and remains intact through the outer journey. It must not determine dominance after identity dissolves. From the granular boundary inward, per-chunk RMS compensation fades in geometrically toward a shared target. The compensation is strictly bounded so noise and extremely quiet captures cannot be amplified without limit. At the horizon, contributors retain pitch, formants and spectral character, while the common plasma—not original recording level—sets their approximate energy.

## Future spectral appetite

APPETITE currently responds to occupancy, onset strength and probability. It does not yet know whether a sound is novel. A future bounded spectral fingerprint could compare incoming material with recent captures: unfamiliar timbre may receive a modest admission advantage, while repeated material remains possible but less likely. Novelty must bias probability rather than become a deterministic duplicate filter.
